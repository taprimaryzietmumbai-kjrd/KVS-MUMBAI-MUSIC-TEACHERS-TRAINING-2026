Welcome

Celebrating music, strengthening practice

This five-day face-to-face professional learning programme brings together Music Teachers of Kendriya Vidyalayas under KVS Mumbai Region at ZIET Mumbai. The hub will serve as a common point for programme information, learning resources, participant contributions and workshop documentation.

Programme information: The dates, venue and mode are confirmed as provided. Session timings, resource-person names, participant roster and links will be added after they are finalised.

Professional learning

Suggested learning strands

Working framework for the training; specific sessions can be mapped to these strands.

01 · Voice and vocal music

Voice culture, breathing, pitch, rhythm, diction, group singing and age-appropriate repertoire.

02 · Indian music traditions

Raga awareness, folk traditions, regional music, devotional and community songs.

03 · Instrumental practice

Instrument familiarisation, accompaniment, rhythm patterns and ensemble coordination.

04 · Music pedagogy

Experiential learning, inclusive participation, listening skills and classroom strategies.

05 · Creativity and composition

Improvisation, song adaptation, movement, creative expression and student compositions.

06 · Assessment and performance

Observation, performance rubrics, reflective practice and documenting musical growth.
