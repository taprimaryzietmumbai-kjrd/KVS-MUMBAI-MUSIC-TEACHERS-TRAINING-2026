Welcome, Participants!

MANDATORY DURING THE WORKSHOP · CPD CERTIFICATE REQUIREMENT

Participants must complete and submit the following three activities during the scheduled workshop programme, within the time allotted for the respective activities.

Completion of all three submissions is mandatory for successful CPD Certificate Generation. Please do not leave any required submission pending at the end of the workshop.
